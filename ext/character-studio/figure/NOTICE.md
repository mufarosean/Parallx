# The figure

`figure.bin` and `figure.json` are generated by
`scripts/bake-character-figure.mjs` from the MakeHuman data that Anny ships
(github.com/naver/anny, commit 8f35dc2d, 2026-10-26):

- the base mesh, body targets, modifier targets and default rig and skin
  weights: MakeHuman assets adapted by MPFB2, CC0 1.0 Universal;
- the two small mesh edits and the way the body targets combine follow
  Anny's code (Copyright 2025 NAVER Corp., Apache License 2.0); none of its
  code is included.

Checked against Anny's own cached rig: 103 bones (all but its root, which
Anny places by its own convention), the template and 1,072 blend shapes
agree to within 0.00001 mm.
