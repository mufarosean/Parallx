Clip Editor redesign

The clip editor becomes a small video editor: the video is big, a real timeline runs along the bottom, one inspector sits on the right, and Export lives in the toolbar. Nothing it does today is removed. The five frames below are mockups; the table at the end lists where each current control goes.

What is wrong today

It is a dialog inside a tab

  • The tab hosts a modal ("Export Clip / GIF") with a hidden close button, fixed at 1180px wide.
  • The video shares a two-column grid with a long control column, so it stays small.
  • A row of hotkeys is printed over the video, permanently.

The timeline is a 32px bar

  • In/Out, segments, crop keyframes and the playhead are squeezed into one strip with 10% ticks.
  • Blur regions and text have no place on the timeline; they are lists of seconds fields.
  • The GIF frame strip is a separate band under everything, with a hidden waveform.

Nine accordions, mixed groups

  • Trim, Output, Crop & motion, Look, Blur, Text, Audio & Finish, Export, Batch queue.
  • Mute and Reverse sit under Look; speed under Output; destination presets under Export.
  • Raw seconds fields (step 0.01), glyph buttons (▶ ✕ ▲ ▼ ⧉), "+ " labels, and --vscode-* fallbacks instead of the kit and tokens.

D. Export from the toolbar

Export is one button with the format and an estimate on it. Its menu holds the destination presets, format, size and quality, with the format-specific options (here GIF) appearing in place. The video track turns into frames for GIF, where per-frame delays and deletions are made.

Narrow panes

Below about 900px the inspector folds into a sheet that slides up over the timeline, opened from the same six tabs shown as an icon row under the transport. Below 640px the timeline drops to the Video and Audio tracks, and the other lanes show as markers on the Video track.

Build order

  1. Shell: drop the dialog; toolbar, stage, transport, inspector tabs and timeline on the kit and --px-* tokens (new values go in a Tier 3 "Clip editor" block). Existing state and export code stay as they are.
  2. Timeline: ruler, Video track with segments, Audio waveform, playhead, zoom. Then the Motion, Blur and Text lanes.
  3. Export menu and queue panel, moving format options out of the inspector.
  4. On-video objects for text, and the GIF Frames mode.
