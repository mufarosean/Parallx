Media Organizer sidebar

Today every section header is the same size and colour as the rows under it, there is nothing between sections, and Folders, Tags and Albums all grow into one another when opened. Below: today's sidebar with what is wrong with it, then three ways to change it. B is the one I'd build.

Drawn from the code as it is. The numbers point at the problems.

What is wrong

  1. Headers look like rows. 12.5px semibold in the secondary text colour, with an icon, against 13px rows with icons. Nothing marks where one section ends: no line, no gap.
  2. The Library header and All Media share an icon (the same "library" mark twice, one under the other).
  3. Clip Projects says the same thing twice when empty: a + in the header and a "New Clip Project…" row under it.
  4. Albums has no icon. It asks for folder-library, which is not in the icon registry, so the header is blank where the icon should be. That is part of why it looks out of place.
  5. Albums is a section of its own, and smart albums are nowhere. Saved searches ("Save As Smart Album") can only be opened from a command's quick pick; they never appear in the sidebar. An album, a smart album and a clip project are all "things I gathered", but they live in three places.
  6. Folders, Tags and Albums compete for height. Each is a long list with its own scroll box (capped at 260px and 320px) and sashes between them, so opening one squeezes the others and you scroll inside small boxes.
  7. Nothing shows where you are. No row is marked when its view is open.
  8. Library mixes two kinds of row: places to look (All Media, Favorites, Recent) and jobs to do (Untagged, Tag Review, Duplicates), plus Trash. Only Tag Review shows a count.

Three ways to change it

All three keep every destination there is today; nothing is taken away, only moved. Hover a frame to see the header actions. Click a section header to fold it, and the Folders | Tags switch in B.

A. Tidy up

Same sections, told apart: quiet labels on a hairline, no header icons, the open view marked.

  • Headers: 11.5px muted label, a hairline and 10px of room above; their buttons show on hover.
  • Row icons stay; header icons go (fixes 1, 2 and 4).
  • Empty Clip Projects: a one-line hint, not a second New row (3).
  • Counts on Untagged and Duplicates; the open view marked (7).
  • Trash moves to the foot, beside Keyboard Shortcuts.
  • Leaves 5 and 6 as they are.

B. Fewer sections Recommended

A's look, plus: Albums, smart albums and clip projects become one Collections list; Folders and Tags share one browser.

  • Top, no header: All Media, Favorites, Recent.
  • To sort: Untagged, Tag Review, Duplicates, each with its count. A row at 0 goes quiet.
  • Collections: albums, smart albums and clip projects in one list, told apart by icon. + offers New Album…, New Smart Album From Search, New Clip Project…. Smart albums show up for the first time (5).
  • Browse: one switch, Folders | Tags, and one list that takes the rest of the height, with its filter always there. No sashes, no boxes inside boxes (6).
  • Drawing and Painting, when it is on, is a section like the others.

C. Tabs

The most compact: four tabs across the top, each owning the whole height.

  • Library | Folders | Tags | Collections.
  • Nothing ever squeezes anything else.
  • But only one kind of thing is in view at a time: dragging photos onto an album from a tag's view needs a tab switch mid-drag, and the To sort counts are hidden while you browse tags.
