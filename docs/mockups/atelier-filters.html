Atelier: the Filters area

Today Filters opens a wide panel under the toolbar. Its three filters sit side by side at different sizes, each laid out its own way, and what is set shows again as a row of chips below it. Below: today, then two ways to set it out. B is the one I'd build.

Today

  1. Three filters, three layouts. Tags is a large block with its own toolbar (Match All/Any, Include sub-tags), a boxed field and a chip cloud. Favorites is a lone button. Date is two boxed text fields. Nothing lines up.
  2. Settings in the header line. Match and Include sub-tags crowd the Tags label, before you have picked a tag they could apply to.
  3. Dates are typed as YYYY-MM-DD, into boxes, with no quick choices. They filter on the date a photo was added to the library, but the panel only says "Date range".
  4. Said twice. What is set appears in the panel and again as chips under the toolbar.
  5. Right-click to exclude is a hidden gesture, explained only by small print.

A. One tidy panel

Filters opens a narrow panel under its button. The three filters are stacked, in one style, with the same quiet fields as the sidebar.

  • Each section has a quiet label. Tag options (Match, sub-tags) sit to the right of the label, small, and only matter once tags are picked.
  • Favorites becomes a switch on one line.
  • Date gets quick choices (Any time, Today, This week, This month, This year, Custom…) and a choice of which date (added or taken).
  • The footer shows how many match, with Clear All.
  • What is set still shows as chips under the toolbar while the panel is closed.

B. Filter pills Recommended

No panel. Under the search there is one pill per filter. An empty pill is dashed and names the filter. A set pill is filled and shows its value, with a × to clear it. Clicking a pill opens a small picker just for that filter. Try them.

  • One place for each filter: the pill is both the control and the readout, so the chip row under the toolbar goes away.
  • Tags: a searchable list with ticks. Click once to include, again to exclude (the box shows a minus), a third time to clear. Excluding is visible, not a hidden right-click. Match and sub-tags are two small options at the top of the list.
  • Favorites: one click toggles it.
  • Date: quick choices, Added or Taken, and Custom… with a calendar.
  • The toolbar's Filters button goes. The pills sit in their own line under the search, so the toolbar keeps one row.
  • "Clear" shows at the end once anything is set.
