  1. No way into the clip editor from the header. The photo page has Edit Image there; the video page has only the chat, favorite and panel icons.
  2. The name is shown twice. The page header and the player's own top bar both say studio-session.mp4.
  3. Four round teal buttons over the picture (save frame, set cover, trim, filmstrip). The colour is not one of the app's, they cover the picture, and only a tooltip says what they do. Trim is the way into the clip editor, hidden as a scissors circle.
  4. Skip and frame step look alike. Chevrons for 5 seconds, triangles for one frame, all the same weight.
  5. In and out are two bookmark icons, one mirrored, next to a loop icon. Nothing on the bar says what in and out are for. The volume slider is always open, and the progress line is thin and only appears on hover.

A. One floating bar, shown on hover

The picture stays clean. Hovering shows one rounded bar at the bottom, the way QuickTime and modern browsers do it: the scrubber with a frame preview on top, then play, step, time, and on the right speed, volume, fullscreen and a ⋯ menu. Everything that is not playback (save frame, set cover, in and out, loop) moves into that menu with its shortcut beside it. Edit Clip goes in the page header, where Edit Image is on a photo. B. A docked bar under the picture Recommended

The clip editor's transport, on the video page. The picture sits alone in the stage; under it, a filmstrip scrubber (frames you can see, with the playhead and any marked range) and one row of controls that never hide. Save Frame and Set Cover are labelled buttons, not floating circles, and In and Out read as what they are. Edit Clip in the header opens the clip editor on the marked range.
