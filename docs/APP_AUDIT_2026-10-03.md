# Whole-app audit, 2026-10-03

The owner asked for a hard look at the whole app: UI gaps, dropdowns that
break the rules, things that look machine-made, and systems that do more work
than they need to, at depth rather than surface.

**How it was done.**

- **A live sweep.** `tests/ui-polish/app-sweep.mjs` drove the real app, in
  dark and in light, with every extension enabled. It covered 87 scenes:
  every activity view, every Open or Show command, every panel tab, the
  palette and the menus. For each scene it measured font sizes, buttons off
  the 22/24/28/32 ladder, accent-filled buttons, native selects, uppercase
  labels and non-core dropdown triggers. Settings, AI Settings and
  Appearance were then probed section by section.
- **Five code audits, run in parallel and read in full.** Each cites
  file:line, and the dropdown audit also reproduced its top findings in the
  app. The reports are in [`docs/audit/2026-10-03/`](audit/2026-10-03):
  - [`audit-dropdowns.md`](audit/2026-10-03/audit-dropdowns.md):
    dropdowns, menus, popovers, dialogs, tooltips and toasts, with a full
    z-index table.
  - [`audit-core-ui.md`](audit/2026-10-03/audit-core-ui.md): the built-ins
    and workbench against the kit and copy rules.
  - [`audit-extensions.md`](audit/2026-10-03/audit-extensions.md): a
    scorecard for each of the eight extensions.
  - [`audit-systems.md`](audit/2026-10-03/audit-systems.md): timers, data,
    rendering, lifecycle, AI calls, file watching.
  - [`audit-duplication.md`](audit/2026-10-03/audit-duplication.md): the
    same thing built several times.

  Line numbers in those reports are against `f9ce86f3` / `b98427ef`; the
  fixes below moved some of them.

**The short version.** The most visible tells are gone from the app: there
are no native `confirm`, `alert` or `select` left, no emoji icons, the type
scale is tame, and light mode holds. What remains is structural:

- **The kit is narrower than what screens need, so screens clone it.**
  About 800 hand-built buttons sit against about 190 kit calls. There are
  roughly 45 chip classes, 10 tab families and 3 empty-state builders.
- **There are several systems for one job.** Settings has two stores and
  two editors, there are four Ollama clients, seven schedulers and up to
  four logs per agent run.
- **Background work used to ignore whether anyone was looking.** The
  biggest single cost, the prompt cache thrown away every chat turn, is
  fixed.

## 2. Still open, ranked

### 2.1 The kit is too small, so every screen clones it (the root cause)

The kit lacks pieces every screen needs, so each screen builds its own, and
the screens drift apart. Everything in 2.2 and most of 2.4 follows from this.

- **What is missing:**
  - a form dialog / side sheet (Media Organizer has about 11 body-mounted
    modals; Budget has 3 drawers; Creations has 5 modals with no Escape);
  - a non-modal toast with an action (today any notification with a button
    becomes a blocking modal, so nothing can offer Undo);
  - toggle, tabs, popover, search input, stat tile, progress bar;
  - a date picker and colour picker (22 native pickers and 2 custom
    calendars give three looks);
  - a tree/sidebar API (about 10 sidebar row styles).
- **The dropdown needs:** icons, descriptions, separators, disabled items,
  typeahead, and a chevron icon instead of the `▾` text glyph. Its open
  animation never runs (the class names in `px-motion.css` don't exist).
- **The menu needs:** keyboard submenus (ArrowRight/Left; today Enter on a
  submenu row closes the menu doing nothing), and header, input and swatch
  rows so canvas's second menu system can move onto it.
- **Tooltips** spill out of their box (no wrapping), never show on keyboard
  focus, and don't hide on scroll.
- **Confirmations are two looks:** 31 go through notification prompts and 16
  are real dialogs.
- **A second type scale.** `--parallx-fontSize-*` / `--parallx-radius-*` sits
  one step below `--px-*` and is used 524 times (43 off scale). Fix with a
  codemod that keeps pixel values, then ban new uses.
- **Nothing guards `src/` the way `extStyleRatchet` guards `ext/`.** The copy
  test reads only `title:`/`label:`/`placeholder:` properties. Extend the
  ratchet to core areas with per-area baselines, and widen what the copy
  test reads.

### 2.2 Screens that read as unfinished or machine-made

- **Creations AI.**
  - The home is a hero question ("Who do you want to meet?") with wand and
    sparkle icons; the Parallx mark is the only AI sign.
  - Under it are four tinted-icon tiles.
  - Its sidebar repeats Home's launchers, has a Settings row and a
    full-width VS Code-blue New Chat.
  - Its own legacy stylesheet carries `#0e639c` buttons, a fixed toast and
    modal at z 1000, and 50 font-family declarations.
- **Concept Lab home.** A landing page: a long intro, a six-tip feature
  grid, "01"–"07" in heavy weight, and Title Case on small words ("Pin A
  Ghost", "Feeds Loss Development As A Process", 71 labels).
- **Dashboard empty state.** A dashed box, an 80px tinted icon tile and
  "Make this yours". There is a lone chevron in the header, and the title
  turns accent on hover (fails contrast in light mode).
- **Chat's "thinking" styling:** glowing orbs and shimmering gradient text.
- **Flashcards.**
  - The header holds only Stats.
  - New Deck (primary), Generate Cards (accent pill) and Import Cards sit
    in a body row.
  - The empty state restates the three buttons.
  - Stats in a zero state is rows of "—" and dashed empty charts.
  - 103 `.fc-btn` and no kit buttons.
- **Atelier.**
  - The sidebar has four empty-hint paragraphs.
  - Its Studio section lists commands that only toast.
  - Library chips come in two styles in one row.
  - Tag Review leads with a large rules textarea on an empty page.
  - 167 hand-built buttons.
- **Welcome.** Shows v0.2.0 (`toolValidator.ts` PARALLX_VERSION) while
  `package.json` says 0.1.0, the title is 28px at weight 300, and the AI rows
  are developer-facing (`/init`, `.parallx/`).
- **Copy.**
  - About 75 action tooltips are in sentence case where the kit uses Title
    Case.
  - AI Settings is named four ways.
  - "Tap" appears in a desktop app.
  - Settings descriptions explain internals ("can't hijack the chat
    picker", "num_ctx").
- **Accessibility.** The Workspace Graph activity icon has an empty
  `aria-label`.

### 2.3 Systems not yet done (from `audit-systems.md`, in payoff order)

1. **Startup blocks the main process.**
   - Docling detection runs synchronous child processes at every launch
     and workspace switch.
   - Four extensions activate on `"*"` (about 2.9 MB of JS; Media Organizer
     alone is 1.95 MB) and are awaited before the loading overlay lifts.
   - Fix: async, cached detection; `onStartupFinished`.
2. **Retrieval runs twice per chat turn** (the memory lane re-embeds and
   re-searches), and MEMORY.md is included twice.
3. **One SQLite worker serves every database**, with no statement cache.
   Reindex deletes by unindexed columns, which is two full scans per page
   save. Chat retrieval queues behind media scans.
4. **Extension AI work runs at chat priority:** budget sync, media tagging,
   flashcard generation. `api.lm` has no priority, abort or timeout, which
   is why there are three stall watchdogs, six model pickers and about 12
   JSON extractors.
5. **Media Organizer.**
   - About 3 database round trips per unchanged file on relaunch (about
     150K for a 50K library).
   - A full grid query per imported file.
   - An undebounced sidebar that leaks two listeners per remount.
6. **Chat streaming** rebuilds the session list and agent rail and re-parses
   the last markdown part on every frame.
7. **About 12 more timers ignore visibility,** with background throttling
   off: two Agents views re-read a log file every 30 s, and the indexing log
   checks at 1 Hz.
8. **Indexing leftovers.**
   - Files skipped as unchanged or empty never get `indexed_at`, so photos
     and scanned PDFs are re-read at every launch.
   - The embedding cache is unbounded (about 630 MB at 100K chunks).
   - Every watcher feeds one change stream; it should route by `watchId`.
9. **Budget.**
   - `readMonthBills` makes one query per bill series.
   - Most ledger listeners have no sequence guard, so an older refresh can
     paint over a newer one.
   - The sync makes 1–3 thinking-model calls per email with no output cap.
10. **Explorer** drops changes that arrive during a refresh.

### 2.4 The same thing, built several times (from `audit-duplication.md`)

- **Time zone.** `chat.timeZone` exists, but budget, web-research,
  media-organizer and workspace-graph hardcode `America/Chicago` at 8
  sites.
- **Markdown and math.** Three pipelines with divergent fixes (the money
  guard only in the shared renderer; `\(…\)` only in chat).
- **Relative time.** 12 copies beside `src/ui/relativeTime.ts`, with five
  wordings and missing the calendar-day fix. One is fixed here (Worksheets).
- **Ollama base URL.** Honoured only by chat; embeddings, the indexer and
  flashcards use localhost, and the CSP allows only localhost.
- **Agent run logs.** One autonomous run is logged in up to four places
  (memory ring, ndjson, hash ledger, run rings).
- **Events.** Three event-bus styles; Media Organizer alone has 24 `mo:*`
  DOM events.
- **Formatters.** Byte sizes 7 copies, money 4, title-casing about 8, HTML
  escaping 16.
- **Settings.** AI settings are edited from two places in one window, with
  different controls and scopes. "Default Model" is a global dropdown in
  AI › Overview and a workspace free-text box in AI › Model.

## 3. Decisions for you

1. **DESIGN_UNIFICATION D1–D5 are still open,** and several findings wait on
   them:
   - the right-edge activity bar (now Chat and Agents);
   - Tools in the activity bar versus Settings › Extensions;
   - tool-owned settings pages (Creations, Workspace Graph) versus app
     Settings only;
   - whether AI Settings stays a panel inside Settings.
2. **Proactive Suggestions:** build the chat cards it was meant for, or
   delete the service. It is off by default now.
3. **Web Research "Ambient" switch** (AI Settings › Web Research): nothing
   reads it. Wire it into the tool gate or remove it.
4. **MIND prediction** keeps learning with the heartbeat off; the storms are
   gone, not the loop. Gate it on the heartbeat if Mind should be part of
   autonomy only.
5. **One time zone:** make `chat.timeZone` (or a renamed app-level setting)
   the only zone and retire the `America/Chicago` hardcodes.
6. **Which version is the app:** 0.1.0 (`package.json`) or 0.2.0 (the shell).

## 4. Suggested order

Each step stands alone, and the order is by payoff for the effort.

1. **Grow the kit:**
   - the dropdown and menu gaps above;
   - form dialog / side sheet;
   - action toast;
   - toggle, tabs, popover, date picker, colour picker;
   - an N-button confirm.

   Then move the 31 notification confirms onto the confirm dialog.
2. **Extend the style ratchet to `src/`** with per-area baselines, and widen
   the copy test. This stops new drift before migrating old.
3. **Codemod `--parallx-fontSize/radius` onto `--px-*`.**
4. **Startup:** async Docling detection; `onStartupFinished` for the four
   `"*"` extensions.
5. **Retrieval once per turn;** bound the embedding cache; `indexed_at` on
   skip.
6. **`api.lm` v2:** priority, abort, timeout, a model picker, JSON parsing.
   Then budget sync, media tagging and flashcards at background priority.
7. **Migrate surfaces onto the kit,** worst first: Creations AI, Atelier,
   Flashcards, Dashboard, Chat composer, Canvas menus.
8. **`api.format` and `api.time.zone()`,** retiring about 60 helpers and the
   hardcoded zone.
9. **Fold the schedulers and logs into tables** (`audit-systems.md` §7).
